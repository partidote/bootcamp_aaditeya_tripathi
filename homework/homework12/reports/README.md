# Deliverable Design & Rationale

### Chosen Audience
* **Primary Stakeholder:** Lead Options Trader & Quantitative Portfolio Manager.
* **Context:** Daily risk meeting and strategy parameter sign-off.

### Format Rationale (Markdown Written Report)
* **Why this fits:** Desk heads require concise, scannable summaries that cleanly pair visual risk-return frontiers with explicit assumptions and concrete operational guardrails.
* **Structure:** Highlights the executive summary first, quantifies scenario sensitivities in a scannable table, and finishes with direct decision implications.
